# Supernova Franchise Copy Guidelines

## Franchise Overview

**Franchise Name:** SUPERNOVA
**Franchise Line:** SUPERCOMFORT. SUPERNOVA
**Category:** Running
**Target Audience:** Everyday runners seeking comfort and support

## Key Messaging

### Franchise Positioning
Supernova is the reliable, easy everyday trainer that harmonizes comfort and support. The franchise focuses on making daily running accessible and comfortable for all levels of runners.

### Core Message Pillars
1. **Supercomfort** - The defining characteristic of the franchise
2. **Daily Running** - Designed for everyday use, not just race day
3. **Harmony of Comfort & Support** - Balance between cushioning and stability
4. **Accessible** - Entry-level to experienced everyday runners

## Technology Terminology

### DREAMSTRIKE+
- **What it is:** Re-engineered super-foam technology in the midsole
- **Benefit:** Ideal balance of comfort and support
- **Approved copy:**
  - "The comfort to keep going"
  - "Comfort that never gets tired"
  - "Super foam engineered for maximum comfort and support"
  - "Mile after mile comfort"

### Support Rods
- **What it is:** Dense bottom-loaded foam system from heel to toe
- **Benefit:** Neutral support and smooth transitions
- **Approved copy:**
  - "Worry-free and super-smooth transitions"
  - "Support system of dense foam rods from heel to toe"

### Comfort Heel Fit
- **What it is:** Multi-layered combination of foam and textile
- **Benefit:** Soft, supportive heel lockdown
- **Approved copy:**
  - "Built to keep heels happy"
  - "Heaven for your heels"
  - "Like the shoe is giving your heel a hug"

### ADIWEAR Outsole
- **What it is:** Durable rubber outsole with strategic cutouts
- **Benefit:** Optimal traction and grip
- **Approved copy:**
  - "Built for stick-to-itivness"
  - "Grip where you need it most"

## Copy Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Focus on comfort as the primary benefit
- Use "everyday" to emphasize daily use
- Reference smooth transitions and support harmony
- Use approved technology names with proper capitalization (DREAMSTRIKE+)
- Keep copy grounded and accessible

### Don'ts
- Don't make performance claims beyond approved technical statements
- Don't position as a racing shoe
- Don't use "revolutionary" or hyperbolic language
- Don't compare to competitor products in consumer-facing copy
- Don't abbreviate technology names
