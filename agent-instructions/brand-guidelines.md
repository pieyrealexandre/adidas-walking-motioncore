# Brand Guidelines - adidas Copy

## Brand Voice
- Authentic and confident
- Community-focused and inclusive
- Performance-driven where relevant
- Culture meets athleticism

## Tone
- Direct and energetic
- Inspirational without being preachy
- Conversational but professional
- Bold and statement-making
- Human, calm, and grounded

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Consumer First**: Focus on what the product brings to them, not about us
2. **Performance When Relevant**: Emphasize technical innovation where applicable
3. **Community**: Highlight collective experience and shared passion
4. **Everyday Life**: Connect products to the consumer's daily routine

## Visual Language in Copy
- Use active, dynamic verbs
- Reference movement and action
- Connect product features to real-life scenarios
- Balance technical specs with emotional resonance

## Do's
- Lead with benefit, follow with feature
- Use short, impactful sentences
- Create urgency without pressure
- Celebrate individuality within the collective
- Focus on feel, comfort, and versatility
- Anchor copy in what they feel, where they wear it, how it moves with them

## Don'ts
- Don't oversell or use excessive hype
- Avoid technical jargon without context
- Don't alienate casual or non-professional athletes
- Avoid clichés and overused sports metaphors
- Never make up strong claims or performance benefits
- Don't try too hard to sound "young" or "cool"
