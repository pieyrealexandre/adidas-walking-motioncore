# Words and Phrases to Avoid

## Overused Sports Clichés
- "Game changer"
- "Next level"
- "Bring your A-game"
- "Rise and grind"
- "No days off"
- "Beast mode"
- "Push your limits"
- "Go the extra mile"

## Generic Marketing Speak
- "Revolutionary"
- "Unprecedented"
- "World-class"
- "State-of-the-art"
- "Cutting-edge" (use sparingly)
- "Premium quality"
- "Luxury"
- "Exclusive" (unless truly limited)
- "Iconic" (minimize usage)
- "Legacy" (minimize usage)
- "Heritage" (minimize usage)

## Vague Descriptors
- "Amazing"
- "Incredible"
- "Awesome"
- "Epic"
- "Insane"
- "Sick" (too informal)
- "Dope" (too casual)
- "Fire" (dated slang)
- "Lit" (dated slang)

## Competitor References
- Never mention competing brands
- Avoid "better than" comparisons
- Don't reference other collaborations

## Words That Undermine Authenticity
- "Basically"
- "Simply"
- "Just"
- "Honestly"
- "Literally" (unless used correctly)
- "Actually"

## Gendered Language
- Avoid "guys" when addressing mixed groups
- Use "players," "athletes," "people" instead
- Don't assume gender in product descriptions

## Problematic Phrases
- "Urban" (has coded connotations)
- "Street cred" (too try-hard)
- "Keeping it real" (dated)
- "On fleek" (outdated slang)
- "No cap" (avoid current slang)
- "Bussin'" (avoid current slang)

## Try-Hard Youth Language
- Avoid slang that sounds forced
- Don't use "online" or meme language
- Trying to sound "young" has the opposite effect
- Keep it natural and timeless

## Overused Product Language
- "Elevate your game"
- "Take it to the next level"
- "Designed for champions"
- "Unleash your potential"
- "Made for winners"
